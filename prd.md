Who plays: One player.
On screen: A basket at the bottom moves left and right with the arrow keys. Stars fall from the top.
Win or lose: Catch 10 stars to win. Miss 3 stars and you lose.

Rock, Paper, Scissors
Who plays: One player against the arcade, or two players taking turns on one device.
On screen: Choose solo or two-player mode. In two-player mode, Player 1 chooses first, then Player 2 chooses without seeing Player 1's move; both moves are revealed after both have chosen.
Win or lose: Rock beats scissors, scissors beat paper, and paper beats rock. Track each player's wins and ties, and reset the score at any time.
